Sprechen-Simulator

telc B1 · Teil 1, 2 und 3 mit Prüfer und Partner · am Ende Bewertung

So funktioniert's

  • The examiner and your partner speak to you. Then it's your turn.
  • Tap 🎤 Sprechen, say your answer in German, tap again to stop. Check the text, then tap Senden. You can also type.
  • Like the real exam: Teil 1 (get to know each other) → Teil 2 (opinions on a topic) → Teil 3 (plan something together).
  • At the end you get a score out of 75 and your corrected sentences.
