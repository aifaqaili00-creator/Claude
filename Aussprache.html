🔊 Aussprache

Tap a button to hear it. Works best in Chrome or Safari, with the sound on.

Tips:
• sp at the start of a word = schp (spreche → "schpreche")
• ch after a/o/u = throat sound, like clearing your throat softly (Sprachen)
• ch after e/i = soft hissing sound, like a cat (spreche, ich)
• -en at the end is short and weak: "Schprachn"
