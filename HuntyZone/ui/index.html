    General

    Theme
    What this means
    Helium 10, product pages and searches open in this profile, with your logins and the Helium 10 extension. The automatic checks use a separate background browser, because Chrome does not let automation tools control your everyday profile.

    Currency

    Your currency
    Your price range is typed in it, and prices from the other marketplaces also show ≈ in it.
    Your supplier quotes in
    Used by Profit? on idea and radar cards and by the profit calculator in keyword reports.
    Radar and idea rows show the most you can pay your supplier per piece and still keep this share of the price.

    Price range for product ideas

    What this means
    Product ideas and the Trend radar show only products that sell on Amazon within this range. It is converted to US dollars, Australian dollars and UAE dirhams automatically with the European Central Bank's daily rates (the dirham is fixed to the US dollar at 3.6725).

    Delivery locations

    Set automatically on the first check. If one stays orange, press Checker browser, click “Deliver to” on that Amazon tab and set it once.

    Checks

    1 page = about 48–60 listings and is the fastest. Saved results are reused, so repeat checks are instant.

    Automatic refresh

    Registered for GST / VAT (changes the profit maths)
    Background work is polite: a few pages at a time, daily limits per marketplace, and it waits whenever you run something yourself. A captcha never pops up in the background; the marketplace just pauses and Home tells you.

    Gap Finder

    “Sells well” means at least this many sold a month
    What this means
    A Best Sellers top 100 place also counts as selling well. The UK and Germany are read only as proof (their Best Sellers lists, a few product pages), never for fees or keyword reports.

    Amazon Selling Partner API (optional)

    What this means
    With your own seller keys the Gap Finder asks Amazon directly who sells a listing (offers, FBA, where it ships from) instead of reading its page. The keys are kept encrypted for your Windows user on this PC only, never in settings.json, and are never shown again. A field left empty keeps the key saved before. Without keys, pages are read as before.

    Go or no-go rules

    What this means
    The deal-breakers and warnings behind Go or no-go in each keyword report (Money). A deal-breaker that fails makes a keyword a No-go whatever its score; a warning lowers Go to Maybe. Set the limits for each market, or switch a rule off. Every decision keeps the rules it was made under, so a report says when a keyword was decided under other rules.

    AI (optional)

    What this means
    Ask AI is off until you save a key. Claude then answers questions about your saved research and board, using read-only tools: it cannot read Amazon pages, click, email or buy. Each question costs a few cents (Health shows this month's total); at the monthly limit Ask AI stops until next month. The key is kept encrypted for your Windows user on this PC only and is never shown again.
